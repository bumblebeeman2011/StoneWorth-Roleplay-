Server Information

StoneWorth Roleplay is a structured and welcoming Flashing Lights community built for players who value realism, teamwork, and a positive environment. We host daily USA‑East servers with active members across all departments, supported by a dedicated staff team that ensures fair, smooth, and enjoyable gameplay.

Our community uses organized Google Docs for rules, ranks, and promotions, keeping everything transparent and easy to follow. We enforce a zero‑tolerance policy for discrimination and provide clear reporting systems to keep every member safe, respected, and heard.

Whether you're here to patrol, respond, train, or simply connect with others, StoneWorth Roleplay is a place where everyone can contribute and grow. We're glad to have you with us.
